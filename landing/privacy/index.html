Privacy policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you visit spikeforge.net and dash.spikeforge.net, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Visit statistics. We count visits with GoatCounter, software we run on our own server. It records the page viewed, the page you came from, your browser and operating system, your screen size, your language, and your country. It does not set cookies and does not store your IP address.
  • Server requests. Our web servers receive your IP address and the details your browser sends whenever a page or file is requested.

Cookies and browser storage

Our sites do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • spikeforge.net keeps your language choice in local storage (spikeforge.locale).
  • dash.spikeforge.net keeps your theme (snn.theme) and language choice (spikeforge.locale) in local storage.

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

Third parties

We do not send visit data to Google or to any advertising or analytics company. Our sites load their fonts from our own servers.

Services that receive some information:

  • Our hosting providers: Hetzner runs the dash.spikeforge.net server and our statistics server; spikeforge.net is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Links to other sites (GitHub and so on) are governed by those sites' own policies.

How we use it

To deliver the sites, keep them secure, and understand how the sites are used. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Block or limit cookies in your browser settings.
  • Use a content blocker to stop visit counting.
  • Ask us for a copy, a correction, or deletion of the information we hold about you.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Capsize-Games/spikeforge/issues with any question about this policy.
